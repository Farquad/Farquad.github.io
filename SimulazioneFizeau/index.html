Light starts from the source (bottom), reflects off the semi-silvered mirror, passes through the toothed wheel (if there's a gap), reaches the distant mirror, and returns. If it finds a gap on return, it reaches the observer. Adjust the wheel speed to find the eclipses where the observer sees no light. The markers indicate the theoretical speeds for the first 5 eclipses based on current configuration.
